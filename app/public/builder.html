Scoring categories

Each question's answers are scored into these categories — they become the bars on the results page. Sieve writes each category's commentary automatically from the respondent's score — write your own low/high message here only if you want to override it.

Questions

Add a question, then give each option a score in whichever categories it applies to. Leave a score at 0 if an option doesn't affect that category. Sieve writes an insight for each answer automatically — add your own only if you want to override it for that specific option.

Personalise each outcome optional

Every respondent's headline, message, and category commentary are already written automatically from their own score — a scorecard works fully out of the box with nothing filled in here. Write in any field below only if you want to override what Sieve generates for that tier, or to add a recommended next step with a real link (the one thing that has to come from you, since nothing here can invent your booking page or WhatsApp number).

Brand Campaign Insight Signal Desk

Turn this on for a brand-sponsored campaign. Captures real audience data — demographics, psychographics, location — and powers the colourful Brand Campaign Insight Report your sponsor sees. Off by default; every field below is optional.

Engagement Campaign Mode Mobile Engagement Agency

Turn this on to run this as a real engagement campaign — shows the respondent their completion time and a shareable result, and surfaces engagement stats (average time spent) in your report.
